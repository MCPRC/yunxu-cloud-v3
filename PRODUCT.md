# 云序 cloud homepage sample

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Vinext + React + TypeScript, selected for the supported Sites workflow. No backend, account registration, payment, or live cloud provisioning is in scope.

## Product Purpose
The user asked for a working website sample inspired by the currently selected jinghanyun.com homepage, following a visual critique. Deliver a complete Chinese cloud-services homepage with a clean white/blue corporate character and refined product selection.

## Users
Working assumption for the sample: developers and business buyers comparing cloud products. This is illustrative positioning, not a real company claim.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Responsive desktop/mobile homepage with the same section topology as the pinned reference; four product categories (ECS, HCS, BMS, SSL); region cards; monthly/annual demonstration pricing; configuration preview and copy in a dialog; solution and FAQ content accessed through navigation; five-region network exploration; demonstration notices and service-support links. Pricing and packages must be clearly marked as demonstration data. Do not invent customer logos, certifications, live performance, SLA, or customer counts.

## Brand Commitments
The user pinned https://www.jinghanyun.com/ as the visual and structural reference. Retain the clear white/blue corporate cloud language, not the exact brand/logo/assets. Yunxu / 云序 is a provisional fictional demonstration brand chosen for this sample, not a claim of affiliation.

## Evidence on Hand
The reference homepage was visually inspected at 1342px viewport width, from hero through footer. Its major improvement opportunities: underfilled region cards, inconsistent tab wrapping, mixed button treatments, and oversized gap in the announcement layout.

## Product Principles
- Every visible control does something useful in the sample.
- Keep pricing and business claims honest and labeled.
- Organize around product understanding and selection.
- Preserve the reference's approachable enterprise character while improving details.

## Structural correction — 2026-11-10

The user explicitly requires the same structure as the reference URL. Preserve this order: compact six-topic navigation; carousel hero; four quick entries overlapping the hero; centered product heading with four tabs, a left introduction/consultation panel and a right two-column region grid; four service advantages in a two-by-two grid; centered global-network heading with left explanations and five regional entries below; announcements with a left agreement panel and right dated list; four service-support entries; four-column footer with a contact panel, technology strip and legal/demo note. Do not reintroduce standalone solution, FAQ or closing-CTA sections. Existing solution, FAQ and configuration interactions stay in their contextual entry points.
